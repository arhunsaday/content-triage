# Privacy

Triage collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere except the site you are triaging:
youtube.com, x.com or instagram.com.

It reads your YouTube Watch Later, X bookmarks or Instagram saved posts, and removes,
re-adds or reorders items in them only when you swipe, press a key or click undo. Those
requests go from that site's tab to the same site, with the session you are already signed
in with. Your cookies are never stored, copied or sent elsewhere.

The extension keeps no history and stores no data. Closing the overlay forgets
everything it showed you.
